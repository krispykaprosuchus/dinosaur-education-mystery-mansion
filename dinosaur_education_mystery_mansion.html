Dinosaur Education
Mystery Mansion

A dinosaur mystery game where your brain is part of the investigation!

20 dinosaurs • 6 roles • grade-level questions • Mystery Mansion map

How to Play

  • Create a lobby or enter a lobby code.
  • Choose a grade level and dinosaur. At least 4 players are required to start.
  • Crewmates answer grade-level questions to complete tasks. Correct answers advance the task bar.
  • Electricians and The Bonker can use vents. Medic can revive knocked-out players.
  • The Bonker and Shadow Bonker try to knock players out. Shadow Bonker becomes invisible for 5 seconds after a knockout.
  • Mr. Yum is neutral and wins by eating enough players.
  • Reporting a knocked-out player and voting at meetings require answering a grade-level question first.
